How I work

01

Understand the business

I started on the front line, doing the work the software supports. I still start every project there, talking with the people who use the system and finding where time and accuracy are lost.

02

Spec it precisely

I turn that knowledge into user stories and acceptance criteria detailed enough that nothing is lost in handoff, down to individual triggers and fields.

03

Build and prove it with AI

I direct AI coding tools to prototype and build, check the results myself, then measure the impact with real data before and after.
